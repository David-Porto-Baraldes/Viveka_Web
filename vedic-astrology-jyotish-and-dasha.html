1. Jyotish: The Sidereal Sky of the Fixed Constellations

In the sacred tradition of India (Jyotish, the Science of Light taught by Maharishi Parashara in the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra), the sky is measured with the Lahiri Chitrapaksha Ayanamsa, subtracting the ~24° precession of the equinoxes to gaze directly at the astronomical constellations as they stand in the firmament.

Through this sidereal lens, Viveka contemplates your Sidereal Sun (Surya), your Sidereal Moon (Chandra), and your Sidereal Ascendant (Lagna), revealing the deeper karmic bone-structure that supports your Western psychological chart.

2. The 27 Lunar Mansions (Nakshatras) & the 120-Year Vimshottari Dasha

For the Vedic sages, the Moon is the queen of perception. As she travels through the zodiac, she rests each night in one of the 27 Lunar Mansions (Nakshatras), each spanning 13°20' of the sky. The exact degree of your birth Moon within its Janma Nakshatra unlocks the most precise timing clock of antiquity: the 120-year Vimshottari Dasha.

Instead of guessing why a decade of your life feels like an expansion of wisdom (Jupiter), a deep pruning of structures (Saturn), or a blossoming of love and art (Venus), Viveka calculates your exact active Mahadasha (great planetary era), Antardasha (sub-period), and Pratyantardasha (present pulse) on the very day you speak with her.

3. Its Vital Function in the Central Sun of the Four Elements

In the Central Sun of the Four Elements, Vedic Jyotish governs your Sidereal Karmic Structure and Vital Clock. Frequently Asked Questions

Why do my signs change in Vedic Astrology (Jyotish)?

Western astrology uses the Tropical zodiac aligned with the vernal equinox, while Vedic Jyotish uses the Sidereal zodiac (Lahiri Ayanamsa) aligned with the fixed background constellations, shifting planetary positions by roughly 24 degrees.

What is the Vimshottari Dasha?

It is a 120-year planetary period system calibrated from the exact degree of your natal Moon in its Nakshatra, showing which planetary energy governs each chapter and sub-chapter of your life.
