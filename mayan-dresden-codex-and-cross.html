1. The Two Sacred Counts of Mayan Time: Heaven and Earth

Many seekers feel confused when they see two different Mayan signatures for the same birth date. Both are true, because they answer two different sacred questions. Homo Universalis is the first living architecture that honors and calculates both simultaneously without mixing or diluting them:

• The Cosmic Signature (Dresden Codex / Classic Long Count GMT 584283 — WHO YOU ARE): Anchored in the astronomical monuments of the Classic Maya, it reveals your 5-position Long Count (Baktun, Katun, Tun, Uinal, K'in), your cosmic Nawal and Tone, your solar Haab' date, your Guardian among the Nine Lords of the Night (Bolon Tiku' G1–G9), the 584-day phase of Venus (Morning or Evening Star), and the 819-day quadrant of K'awiil.
• The Earthly Signature (Oral K'iche' Cholq'ij Tradition — HOW YOU WALK): Preserved uninterruptedly by the highland Ajq'ijab' daykeepers of Guatemala, it reveals the Nawal and Tone of your daily action, human service, and community walk on Earth, along with your Year Bearer (Mam).

2. The Five-Point Mayan Cross (La Cruz Maya)

A human being is never a single Nawal in isolation; you are a Mayan Cross of five energies. At the center stands the Nawal of your birth; above your crown shines the Nawal of your Conception (9 trecenas before birth, how your spirit descended); at your feet rests your Destiny and Mission (8 trecenas forward); to your left, your ancestral feminine hemisphere and inner gift; to your right, your masculine action and material manifestation. Viveka contemplates both your Cosmic Dresden Cross and your Earthly K'iche' Cross.

In Mayan cosmology, the twenty Nawals are woven across the four cosmic directions and colors: East (Red / Fire) of dawn and initiation, North (White / Air) of ancestral breath and wisdom, West (Black / Earth) of introspection and gestation, and South (Yellow / Water) of ripening and abundance. What is the Mayan Cross of five Nawals?

The Mayan Cross maps five Nawals around your birth: Center (birth), Conception (above), Destiny (below), Left arm (spiritual/ancestral support), and Right arm (material action and relationships).
